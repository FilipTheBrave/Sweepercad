Share a part

Made something in SweeperCAD others could use? Share it here — no account needed. A robot checks it and draws its picture, and a minute or so later it is in the library.

Drop a .sweeperasset or .sweeper file here, or tap to choose one

In SweeperCAD: select the part and choose Share online… — or use any asset from your shelf folder, or a saved project.

What gets checked: it must be a real SweeperCAD file containing only shapes (nothing that runs, no pictures, no imported meshes yet), it must build into a solid, and the name and text must be decent. Anything you didn't type — file paths, reference images, notes — is removed before it is published. Nothing about you is stored.
