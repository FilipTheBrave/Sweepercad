SweeperCAD

The simplest modeller for the most complex shapes.

Download for Mac (Apple Silicon) Mac (Intel) Windows

Free to use. Mac: macOS 12.3 or later. The first time you open it: on a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open (on macOS 15 and later: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway); on Windows, unzip it, run SweeperCAD.exe and choose More info → Run anyway. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Apple M…" is Apple Silicon, "Intel" is Intel.
